Temperature Sensing & Power Notes

  • temperature_sensor_status = 2: In this report, interpreted as temperature sensor not installed. At the protocol layer this maps to raw value 0xFFFF (official manual: "collection failed"). When the optional NTC cable temperature sensor is not connected, the device continuously reports this status.
  • Shared Type-C port: CT103 USB Type-C is used for ToolBox configuration/debugging and the optional cable temperature sensor — not simultaneously. Without the probe, periodic packets typically include temperature_sensor_status but not temperature.
  • Induced-current startup threshold: Per CT103 specs, minimum measured current is about 12 A at 1-minute reporting (about 6 A at 10-minute reporting). Below the induced-power threshold, the device may stop periodic uplinks or enter low-power/low-voltage mode.
  • Type-C external power: Supplying 5 V via Type-C allows continued reporting even when measured current is below threshold; however this is not Milesight's recommended approach (designed for induced self-power; Type-C is primarily for configuration and optional temperature sensing).
  • This dataset: 2/44 packets include cable temperature readings; 42/44 show status=2 (not installed), indicating the temperature sensor was connected only during a short test window.
